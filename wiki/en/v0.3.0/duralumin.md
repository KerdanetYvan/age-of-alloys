# Duralumin

Duralumin is the alloy of **miners in a hurry**: it mines faster than steel, but wears out faster than iron. In real life it is an aviation alloy: mostly aluminum, with a little copper, magnesium and manganese.

## Making it

It is melted in [the foundry]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/foundry/' | relative_url }}), with a **heat 2** fuel (at least a coal): see [the recipe]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/recipes/' | relative_url }}#alloys). You need all four metals: see [the ores]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/ores/' | relative_url }}). Magnesium and manganese need an iron-level pickaxe.

## Tools and weapons

All of them mine at iron level. Shapes and amounts: [the recipes]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/recipes/' | relative_url }}).

| | Duralumin | _Iron (reference)_ |
|---|---:|---:|
| Durability | 200 | _250_ |
| Mining speed | 7.5 | _6_ |
| Enchantability | 15 | _14_ |
| Sword damage | 4.5 | _6_ |
| Sword attack speed | 2.0 | _1.6_ |

The sword hits more often (2.0 times per second) but less hard.

## Armor

It protects like iron armor, but wears out fast. It is the easiest to enchant well.

| Piece | Protection | Durability |
|---|---:|---:|
| Helmet | 2 | 110 |
| Chestplate | 6 | 160 |
| Leggings | 5 | 150 |
| Boots | 2 | 130 |
| **Full set** | **15** | **550** |

Toughness: 0. Enchantability: 15. For comparison, iron has 15 protection and 825 durability for the full set.

Repair it at an anvil with a duralumin ingot.
