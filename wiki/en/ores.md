# The ores

Four metals are found in the world: **tin**, **aluminum**, **magnesium** and **manganese**. Each one exists as a **stone** ore and as a **deepslate** ore, and generates in **every biome of the Overworld**.

> **Important:** ores only generate in chunks created with the mod. Create a new world, or go to an area you have never explored.

## Where to dig

The height (Y) gives the zone; veins are more numerous in the middle of the zone and rarer towards its edges.

| Ore | Height (Y) | Veins per chunk | Vein size | Minimum pickaxe |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Tin | -24 to 80 | 16 | 9 | stone |
| Aluminum | 0 to 128 | 14 | 9 | stone |
| Magnesium | -48 to 48 | 4 | 6 | iron |
| Manganese | -64 to 16 | 6 | 6 | iron |

- **Aluminum**: the most common, from the surface down to the caves.
- **Tin**: almost as common as copper.
- **Magnesium**: rare, around Y = 0.
- **Manganese**: rare and deep, mostly below Y = 0.

"Iron-level pickaxe" means iron, bronze, duralumin or steel: they all mine at iron level.

## What an ore drops

Each ore drops its **raw item** (raw tin, raw aluminum, etc.), with more items under Fortune. With Silk Touch, you get the **block** itself.

## Smelting into ingots

The raw item and both ore blocks smelt into an ingot:

| Where | Time |
|---|---:|
| Furnace | 10 s |
| Blast furnace | 5 s |

The ingots are then used to make the alloys in [the foundry]({{ '/en/foundry/' | relative_url }}).
