# How to play

From the first ingot to steel armor, step by step. Everything is played in **survival**. To install the mod, see [the home page]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/' | relative_url }}#installation).

> **Important:** ores only generate in **chunks created with the mod**. Create a new world, or go to an area you have never explored.

## Step by step

1. **Find tin and aluminum** in stone (a stone pickaxe is enough), and Minecraft's **copper**. See [the ores]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/ores/' | relative_url }}).
2. **Smelt** the raw items into ingots, in a furnace or a blast furnace.
3. **Craft the foundry** at a crafting table: its recipe is in [the recipes]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/recipes/' | relative_url }}#foundry).
4. **Make [bronze]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/bronze/' | relative_url }})**: 3 copper ingots and 1 tin ingot, with any fuel.
5. With an iron-level pickaxe, **look for magnesium and manganese** (deeper) for **[duralumin]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/duralumin/' | relative_url }})**.
6. For **[steel]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/steel/' | relative_url }})**, you need iron, coal and a very hot fuel (a coal block or a lava bucket).
7. **Craft** tools, weapons and armor from the ingots: [the recipes]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/recipes/' | relative_url }}).

## Good to know

- **Pickaxe level.** "Iron level" means iron, bronze, duralumin or steel: all of them mine at iron level. Magnesium and manganese need one.
- **Heat.** Each alloy needs a hot enough fuel: any fuel for bronze, a coal for duralumin, a coal block or a lava bucket for steel. A fuel that is too cool is not consumed. See [the foundry]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/foundry/' | relative_url }}).
- **Recipes unlock** as soon as you own an ingot of the metal (or stone bricks for the foundry).
- **Fortune and Silk Touch** work on the ores: more raw items with Fortune, the block itself with Silk Touch.
- **Creative mode**: everything is in the "Age of Alloys" tab.

## Which alloy to pick?

- **Bronze**: the easiest to get and sturdy, with more than twice the durability of iron.
- **Duralumin**: the tool of miners in a hurry, which mines the fastest but wears out fast, and has the best enchantability.
- **Steel**: the best, just below diamond, but it needs the hottest fuel.

The comparison numbers are in [the items]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/items/' | relative_url }}).
