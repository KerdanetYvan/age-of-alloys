# Age of Alloys

**The best of every metal.** Age of Alloys is a Minecraft mod (Forge 1.20.1) that adds **alloys**, and with them new tools, weapons and armor. You find the metals in the world, mix them in a **foundry**, and forge **bronze**, **duralumin** and **steel**, each with its own [character]({{ '/en/v0.4.0/alloys/' | relative_url }}).

> **State of the mod:** testing pre-release (v0.4.0). Everything can be played in survival, with a guide in the game (the advancements) and a recipe book for the foundry, but expect rough edges. Compatibility with other mods comes later.

## Installation

You need Minecraft **1.20.1** and **Forge 47.x** (Java 17).

1. Install Forge for Minecraft 1.20.1.
2. Download the file `ageofalloys-<version>.jar` from the [releases](https://github.com/KerdanetYvan/age-of-alloys/releases) page.
3. Put it in the `mods` folder of the game.

Then head to [How to play]({{ '/en/v0.4.0/how-to-play/' | relative_url }}).
