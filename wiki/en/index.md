# Age of Alloys

**The best of every metal.** Age of Alloys is a Minecraft mod (Forge 1.20.1) that adds **alloys**, and with them new tools, weapons and armor. You find the metals in the world, mix them in a **foundry**, and forge **bronze**, **duralumin** and **steel**.

> **State of the mod:** testing pre-release (v0.3.0). Everything can be played in survival, but expect rough edges. Compatibility with other mods, advancements and a recipe book come later.

## Installation

You need Minecraft **1.20.1** and **Forge 47.x** (Java 17).

1. Install Forge for Minecraft 1.20.1.
2. Download `ageofalloys-<version>.jar` from the [releases](https://github.com/KerdanetYvan/age-of-alloys/releases) page.
3. Put it in the `mods` folder of your game.

## Getting started in survival

Ores only generate in **chunks created with the mod**: create a new world, or go to an area you have never explored.

1. **Find tin and aluminum** in stone (a stone pickaxe is enough), and Minecraft's **copper**. See [the ores]({{ '/en/ores/' | relative_url }}).
2. **Smelt** the raw items into ingots, in a furnace or a blast furnace.
3. **Craft the foundry** at a crafting table: [the foundry]({{ '/en/foundry/' | relative_url }}).
4. **Make [bronze]({{ '/en/bronze/' | relative_url }})**: 3 copper ingots and 1 tin ingot, with any fuel.
5. With an iron-level pickaxe, **look for magnesium and manganese** (deeper) for **[duralumin]({{ '/en/duralumin/' | relative_url }})**.
6. For **[steel]({{ '/en/steel/' | relative_url }})**, you need iron, coal and a very hot fuel (a coal block or a lava bucket).
7. Craft tools, weapons and armor: [the recipes]({{ '/en/recipes/' | relative_url }}).
