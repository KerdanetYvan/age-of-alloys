# Items

Everything the mod adds, by category, with the numbers to compare. The _iron_ and _diamond_ references are Minecraft's. Everything is in the "Age of Alloys" creative tab.

## The alloys

| Alloy | In short | Page |
|---|---|---|
| **Bronze** | Sturdy, iron that lasts longer | [Bronze]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/bronze/' | relative_url }}) |
| **Duralumin** | Fast but fragile, very enchantable | [Duralumin]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/duralumin/' | relative_url }}) |
| **Steel** | The best, just below diamond | [Steel]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/steel/' | relative_url }}) |

Each alloy gives **5 tools and weapons** (pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe, sword) and **4 armor pieces** (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots). The recipes are in [the recipes]({{ '/en/v0.3.0/recipes/' | relative_url }}).

## Tools and weapons

All of them mine at iron level.

| | Bronze | Duralumin | Steel | _Iron_ |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Enchantability | 12 | 15 | 9 | _14_ |
| Sword damage | 6 | 4.5 | 6.5 | _6_ |
| Sword attack speed | 1.6 | 2.0 | 1.6 | _1.6_ |

## The ingots

Seven ingots: the three alloys (**bronze**, **duralumin**, **steel**) and the four metals of the world (**tin**, **aluminum**, **magnesium**, **manganese**). Other mods recognize them through their tags (`forge:ingots/<metal>`).
